Our Story

In 2011, Mr. Nurulla noticed that many road accidents were happening in Andhra Pradesh. He realized that the main reason for these accidents was that people did not know how to drive safely.

He wanted to fix this problem. He made it his mission to train people the right way and produce excellent drivers. His big goal was to reduce road accidents and make Andhra Pradesh a safer place for everyone.

With this dream, he started a driving school. He named it Imran Driving School after his son, Imran.
